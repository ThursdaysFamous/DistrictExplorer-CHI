districtry / design preview
Unlisted design review. This page is not linked from the app, not indexed, and not in the sitemap. Everything here is mockup and sample data — the live product is Chicago District Explorer. Nothing on the live site changes until each adoption step is approved.

Design canvases

App shellThe rebranded app in every card state — light/dark, live 69-county coverage geometry, layer toggles, loading / empty / error cards. Brand specMark 5c, wordmark rules, the three-tier color architecture, type & spacing, fleet naming, icon asset table. Logo wireframesFive directions and the record of why 5c carried — with a tweakable polygon construction playground. Direction reviewDecisions of record: the name, fleet-by-state scope, the mark, the color split, deliverables index. OG cardThe 1200×630 social card at real size.

The canvases are interactive design documents — they load React from unpkg at view time, so a network-restricted browser shows a blank page. Stats in the mockups are hand-set snapshots; the coverage map itself is fetched live from the app's data.
