The changelog

  • 3 September 2026

    County commissioner districts

    The layer Michigan was chosen for: all 619 county commissioner districts across all 83 counties, from the Bureau of Elections' own statewide compilation of the plans counties file under MCL 46.401-46.405. Every Michigan county elects its commissioners by single-member district, so every reader in the state gets a district. The card names the district, its county and the population it was apportioned on — and not the commissioner. The state's layer does carry a name per district, but it lists the winners of the November 2024 election rather than whoever holds the seat now: checked against the counties' own board pages it is 93.5% right, every error runs the same direction, and one district still names a commissioner who died in June 2025. Naming people from it would have been easy and wrong, so this app says what it knows and records the rest as a gap.

  • 3 September 2026

    Arrived

    Four layers, statewide, each with a sourced boundary: county (83), U.S. House (13), Michigan Senate (38) and Michigan House (110). Both chambers and the U.S. House delegation carry a weekly-refreshed roster; the county card names the county and claims nothing about who runs it, because Michigan's commissioner districts ship as their own change rather than half-joined here. The congressional boundary is the first in the fleet built against TIGERweb's 120th-Congress layer — the retired CD119 field now 400s outright.
